Crave Privacy Policy

Food & Grocery Delivery Service • Effective Date: November 2026

Google Play Data Safety & GDPR / CCPA Compliant

At CraveCourier ("we", "our", or "us"), operated under com.cravecourier.app, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your personal data when you use the CraveCourier customer and delivery partner applications.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, encrypted password hash, and user role (Customer, Delivery Partner, Vendor, Admin).
  • Location Data (Precise and Coarse):
    • Customers: Used to display nearby restaurants and marts, check delivery radiuses, and calculate accurate delivery fees and ETAs.
    • Delivery Partners (Riders): Foreground and background GPS location tracked during active delivery shifts to calculate optimal order auto-dispatch, route navigation, and live customer tracking via Socket.io.
  • Payment & Financial Data: Payment card details, billing address, and transaction histories are securely transmitted directly to our PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processor (Stripe). We never store raw debit/credit card numbers or CVVs on our servers.
  • Device & Notification Data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens, browser push subscriptions, IP address, device model, and operating system version for real-time delivery tracking alerts.
  • KYC Documents (Delivery Partners Only): Driver's license, vehicle registration, and identity proofs uploaded securely for partner onboarding verification.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your food and grocery orders.
  • To provide real-time GPS tracking and live status updates (Placed → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready → Out for Delivery → Delivered).
  • To facilitate customer support, order cancellations, and refunds.
  • To prevent fraud, verify delivery completion via one-time passwords (OTP), and protect platform integrity.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share necessary data only with trusted infrastructure partners:

  • Stripe: Payment processing and dispute resolution.
  • Google Firebase / FCM: Push notifications for live order and dispatch notifications.
  • Mapbox / Google Maps API: Map geocoding, reverse geocoding, and rider navigation.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Encrypted cloud database storage.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable tax and legal obligations.

Right to Delete: You can request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time directly through the app profile settings or by emailing privacy@cravecourier.in. Upon receipt, all identifiable records and FCM tokens are purged within 30 days.

5. Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS 1.3), hashed passwords with bcrypt (salt rounds: 12), secure httpOnly cookies, and role-based access control (RBAC).
